# FIFA 16 R98e: Player Career

Player Career for the R98e pt-BR build. You can create your own player, or play as a player from your club's squad. Your
player develops, earns (or loses) the manager's trust, gets transfer offers, and the whole career saves and loads.

**No APK change is needed.** Everything here is UX (Lua + one Prism scene). Your current
`FIFA16-V7-R98e-PRISM4-PT-BR-SIGNED.apk` works as it is.

## Install

Copy `ux/` over your R98e `ux/` folder (same paths; 17 files replaced, 2 new). Nothing else changes.

## What you get

**Main menu → Career tab → Player Career** (next to Career Mode). Then:

1. Pick your club (the usual team picker).
2. Choose **Create Your Player** or **Play as a Squad Player** (the second is the old PlayerSelector).
3. **Create Your Player** runs on the Career Setup screen, with a live 3D model:
   - **Player**: Position (13), Age (16–30), Starting Level (45–80 OVR), Potential (65–95)
   - **Attack**: Pace, Shooting, Passing, Dribbling focus (Weak … Elite)
   - **Defence**: Defending, Physical focus
   - **Look**: Skin Tone (7), Face, More Faces (19,407 faces, youngest first)

   The focus rows move attribute points between areas. The overall always equals the Starting Level you chose,
   calculated with the game's own formula (`PlayerDev.formula`).

### Why the look works the way it does

In this game almost every player has his own head, face and hair model. The database's high-quality-head flag is set
for 57,898 of 57,916 players, and libpv loads `head_<playerId>_0.rx3`. The generic hair, beard and skin codes mostly
don't drive anything. So your face *is* a real player model. The faces come from PrismYouth's host bodies (players at
clubs in leagues outside the career). The body becomes a youth of kind `C` at your club (`PrismYouth.adoptPro`), so in
matches you are that model: no database writes and no native hooks. Attributes reach real matches through the engine's
match override table, the same path all development uses.

### In the career

- **AI manager picks the team** (existing `PlayerCareer.pickAISquad`). **Manager trust** (0–100) decides how often you
  start, within your own line only. Each match moves trust by your rating, man-of-the-match and red cards. Each month it
  drifts toward what your overall is worth in the squad. Rotation games give a fringe player his chance. Roles: Key
  Player, First Team, Rotation, Squad Player, Fringe Player.
- **Not in the XI?** The match is simulated (existing behaviour).
- **Stats**: apps, starts, minutes, goals, assists, MOTM, clean sheets and average rating. Kept for the career, the
  season, the last 10 matches, and one line per finished season.
- **Growth**: PlayerDev tracks you from day one. You grow, peak and decline like everyone else (age, minutes, form,
  training).
- **Offers for you**: asked on the hub when it goes idle. **Accept** moves you to the new club and the career follows
  you. **Reject** drops the offer. **Later** keeps it for the **My Offers** card.
- **My Player**: the Prism Player Profile screen on your player.
- **Retirement**: the season-end retirement roll never retires your player.
- **Saves**: written as `mode = "player"` plus a `pro` block. They load from the Career tab, and when manager and player
  saves sit in the same tab, both Load tiles show.

## Bugs fixed on the way

- Player-career saves were written as `mode = "manager"`, and the player was never stored, so a player career reloaded
  as a manager career.
- `PlayerCareer.pickAISquad` could drop a player from the squad completely when the bench was full (it removed him
  after he had already left the position lists).
- Nothing in the main menu opened Player Career.

## Porting into your source repo

Some of these files are generated: `Popup.skin.lua` (from `src/skins/Popup.skin.js`), `PrismI18nData.lua` (from
`i18n/*.json`), and `*__*.lua` (the skin-wrapped copies). The scene edit can be re-applied after a regen with
`python3 tools/pro_popup_pn.py ux/prism/skins/Popup.pn` (idempotent). The rest belongs in your `src/patches` /
`src/career` the same way PrismYouth's hooks are. Send the source repo and I'll do the port.

## Not done: Player Lock on your player

`fifa:f16lock:1` locks you to a **random** player, and this variant forces Player Lock off in every `Match.lua`. From
the shipped `libfifa.so` (read-only disassembly):

- The receiver (`0x2c38d20`) stores the value under key `0xeb6154c6` and zeroes the per-side slot keys `0xcff6c26e`
  (side 0) and `0xcf7c4977` (side 1).
- The picker (`0x2dbc000`) locks onto the player whose `[player+0x34]` equals the side key − 1, and only picks at random
  when the key is 0.

Smallest fix: in the receiver, `f16lock:N` with N ≥ 2 → lock on, and both side keys = N − 1 (slot N − 2). The Lua side
would then send `fifa:f16lock:<slot + 2>` in player-career matches. This needs your libfifa patch builder
(`build_r56.py`) and one on-device check that `[player+0x34]` is the lineup slot.
