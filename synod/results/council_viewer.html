How the council took the 40-question exam

Each question goes to four members. Two are symbolic (squares) and two are neural (circles). Pick a question to see how it was read, what each member privately worked out, who spoke up, who checked whom, and how the final call was made. The council was trained on the curriculum only and frozen for the exam.

How to read the numbers

Calibrated confidence is a member's own estimate, learned from its track record in similar situations. The council's reading is the same claim as the other members see it, after their trust in that member. Belief is the council's current probability that an answer is right. It moves with every review, and an answer is only delivered if its belief clears the line on the meter (0.5 here: a wrong answer costs as much as a right one earns). Rival answers can't both be right, so their beliefs are weighed against each other at the end.
