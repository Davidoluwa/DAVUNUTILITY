How to read a step

Every expert in the room proposes what it thinks the next step is. Experts proposing the same step back the same candidate. The number next to each backer is how sure it is, from its own track record on unfamiliar ground. Reviewers report on the result (checks passed or failed, whether the size looks right, which words of the question the step accounts for). The judge, a model trained on how these signals turned out, gives each candidate a probability, including "none of these". The room keeps the three most probable partial solutions open; routes that reach the same answer pool. It answers only if the answer's probability reaches 0.5.
